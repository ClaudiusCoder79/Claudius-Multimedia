Claudius Multimedia 1.0.0 - music & video player for Windows 11 (64-bit)
Created by Michal Czapla & Claude AI.  Freeware (GNU GPL v3).
https://github.com/ClaudiusCoder79/Claudius-Multimedia

GETTING STARTED
  1. Library > Add folder: choose the folders with your music and videos.
     Claudius reads the tags and covers and shows your albums.
  2. Double-click an album or a track to play it. Drop files or folders on the window
     to play them straight away (hold Ctrl to add them to the play queue instead).
  3. Videos switch the window to the video view. Subtitles (.srt / .vtt) with the same
     name as the video are loaded automatically; Home > Subtitles loads others.

TABS
  File      open files, folders, URLs; load / save play lists; settings
  Home      the most used buttons: open, play, audio track, subtitles, snapshot, covers, lyrics
  Playback  shuffle, repeat, jump 10 s, speed, volume
  Library   add folders, browse by artist / album / genre / track, batch covers and lyrics, playlists
  Tags      tag editor, MusicBrainz, tags from file name, covers, rename files from tags
  Lyrics    find / edit lyrics, load .lrc, text size, timing
  Playlist  the play queue: add, remove, shuffle, sort, save
  View      System / Light / Dark theme, language, panels, full screen, mini player, always on top
  About     version, authors, freeware notice, licences

COVERS AND LYRICS
  Find cover searches iTunes, Deezer and MusicBrainz / Cover Art Archive.
  Find lyrics searches LRCLIB; synced lyrics scroll with the music - click a line to jump.
  Library > Missing covers / Missing lyrics fetch them for many tracks at once.
  Only artist, album, title and length are sent. Switch this off in Settings > Online.

KEYBOARD
  Space play/pause   Left/Right seek 5 s   Ctrl+Left/Right seek 30 s   Up/Down volume
  M mute   N / P next / previous   S stop   [ ] speed   F11 or Enter full screen
  Esc leave full screen / mini player   Ctrl+M mini player   Ctrl+O open   Ctrl+U open URL
  Ctrl+T tag editor   Ctrl+L lyrics   Ctrl+F search   F5 rescan   F12 snapshot

WHERE THINGS ARE KEPT
  Program:  C:\Program Files (x86)\Claudius Multimedia
  Settings, library database, cover cache:  %APPDATA%\Claudius Multimedia
  Video snapshots:  Pictures\Claudius Multimedia (change it in Settings > Playback)
  Translations:  lang\*.json in the program folder (en, pl, de, fr, it, es)

UNINSTALL
  Start > All apps > Claudius Multimedia > Uninstall Claudius Multimedia,
  or Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. Your music and video files are never deleted.
