Online services
---------------
Covers, lyrics and MusicBrainz tags are looked up only when you ask for them, or - when
"Download lyrics automatically" is on in Settings > Online - for the track that is playing.
Only artist, album, title and track length are sent, to:
  - LRCLIB            https://lrclib.net           (lyrics)
  - MusicBrainz       https://musicbrainz.org      (tags, data CC0)
  - Cover Art Archive https://coverartarchive.org  (covers)
  - iTunes Search API https://itunes.apple.com     (covers)
  - Deezer API        https://api.deezer.com       (covers)
Turn all of this off in Settings > Online. Cover images and lyrics belong to their owners;
they are downloaded for your personal use.
